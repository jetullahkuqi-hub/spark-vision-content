# Spark Vision – brand rules

Spark Vision is the owner's own marketing studio (social media management, content creation, ads, strategy, reporting).
Instagram: **@spark_visionn** · hashtag **#sparkvision** · phone **+383 49 297 204** (Kosovo).
Bio line: "Growing brands through content." Sister account: @sparkvisionn.swiss.

## Language
- All post designs, Reels and captions are in **English**. (The owner talks to Claude in Albanian; reports to him are in Albanian.)

## Look
- Background: deep navy gradient `#060e2a → #030817`, faint dot grid, one soft blue glow.
- Accent: electric blue `#3D7BFF` (one accent word or element per design, never more than a few).
- Text: white `#ffffff` for headlines; `#c9d6f7` body; `#a9bdf0` small labels.
- Fonts: **Barlow Condensed** 800 uppercase for headlines, 600 spaced uppercase for labels; **Barlow** 400–600 for body. Files in `brand/fonts/`.
- Logo: `brand/logo-full.png` (mark + SPARK VISION wordmark, white/blue, transparent) top-left; `brand/logo-mark.png` for the S mark alone.
- Every feed post: logo top-left, small outlined pill label top-right, footer line with `# sparkvision` left and phone right (or a short tagline).
- Format: feed posts 1080×1440 (3:4); Reels 1080×1920 (9:16).

## TikTok (different from Instagram)
- The owner wants **different, funny videos on TikTok** – not the Instagram Reels.
- Humour: relatable agency / client / small-business-owner moments (client requests, the algorithm, posting consistency,
  explaining the job). Punch up, never down; no real people, clients or brands; nothing mean about anyone's looks, origin etc.
- Formats: `meme` (caption on top + licensed stock reaction clip), `chat` (DM conversation with typing dots and pop sounds) and
  `text` (kinetic text), all via `tools/make_tiktoks.py`.
- Stock footage: ONLY Mixkit clips with the **Mixkit Stock Video Free License** (see `stock/SOURCES.md`). Never download or repost
  other people's videos from YouTube/TikTok/Instagram — copyright and account-strike risk. Owner agreed to this approach.
  Try new formats when the data says so.
- Captions: short, lowercase, casual, one emoji is fine, a question or "tag someone" CTA, 5–7 hashtags incl. #sparkvision.
- Keep important text away from the right edge (TikTok's buttons) and the bottom ~300 px (TikTok's caption).

## Content rules
- No prices. No invented statistics, client names, results, testimonials or reviews.
- No emojis inside designs (captions may use a few).
- Never use other brands' logos, characters or copyrighted music. Reel music is generated by `tools/music.py`.
- Tone: confident, short sentences, practical. Chess/strategy metaphor is part of the brand ("Strategy that delivers", "Every move has a purpose", "Focus. Consistency. Results.").
